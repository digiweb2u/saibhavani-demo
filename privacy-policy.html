Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

SAI BHAVANI TOURS & TRAVELS (“we”, “us”), Karnatakanagepalli, Puttaparthi, Sri Sathya Sai District, Andhra Pradesh 515134 respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website or book with us, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number and email address.
  • Trip and ticket details: pickup and drop locations, dates and times, flight or train numbers, passenger names, ages and ID details needed for tickets, number of passengers and luggage.
  • Payment details: payment reference and status, so we can match payments and process refunds.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited.

2. Why we use it

  • To confirm and carry out your booking and share driver and vehicle details.
  • To book tickets with airlines, IRCTC or bus operators.
  • To process payments and refunds.
  • To send booking updates and respond to queries and complaints.
  • To keep records required by law, such as tax and invoice records.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

3. Who we share it with

  • Our drivers, only the details needed to complete your trip.
  • Airlines, IRCTC, bus operators and hotels, only the details needed for your booking.
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities, when required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, after which they are deleted.

5. Security

Our website uses HTTPS encryption. Access to your information is limited to people who need it to serve you.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@saibhavanitoursandtravels.com. Some records must be kept to meet legal obligations.
