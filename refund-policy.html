Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy applies to all bookings paid fully or partly in advance to SAI BHAVANI TOURS & TRAVELS.

1. Vehicle bookings: cancellation by you

When you cancelRefund of amount paid
More than 24 hours before pickup100% refund
Between 6 and 24 hours before pickup50% refund
Less than 6 hours before pickup, or no-showNo refund

2. Temple and holiday packages

When you cancelRefund of amount paid
More than 7 days before the trip100% refund, minus non-refundable hotel or entry bookings already made
Between 2 and 7 days before the trip50% refund
Less than 2 days before the trip, or no-showNo refund

3. Flight, train and bus tickets

Ticket cancellations and refunds follow the rules of the airline, IRCTC or bus operator. We refund whatever amount the provider returns, minus our service charge, within 5–7 working days of receiving it from the provider. Our service charge is not refundable once the ticket is booked.

4. Cancellation by us

If we cancel a booking for any reason or cannot provide the vehicle, you get a 100% refund of the amount paid to us.

5. Changes to a booking

You can change the date, time or pickup point of a vehicle booking free of charge up to 12 hours before pickup, subject to availability. A change of vehicle may change the fare.

6. Trips cut short

If you end a trip early by choice, the fare for the booked distance or minimum kilometres still applies. If a trip ends early because of a breakdown and we cannot arrange a replacement, you pay only for the distance travelled and the balance is refunded.

7. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of the cancellation.
  • The money goes back by UPI or bank transfer.
  • Your bank may take a further 3–5 working days to show it in your account.

8. How to cancel

Call or WhatsApp +91 89780 59618, or email info@saibhavanitoursandtravels.com, with your name and booking date. The cancellation time is the time we receive your message.
