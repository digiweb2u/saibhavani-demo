About us

A Puttaparthi-based taxi and tour operator, rated 4.8 on Google.

Sai Bhavani Tours & Travels is a taxi and tour operator based in Puttaparthi, Andhra Pradesh,. We provide airport transfers, outstation trips, temple and holiday packages, corporate transport, and flight, train and bus ticket booking for visitors and residents of Puttaparthi and the surrounding region.

Many of our guests are devotees visiting Prasanthi Nilayam and families travelling to and from Bengaluru Kempegowda International Airport. We also cover Tirupati, Srisailam, Lepakshi, Horsley Hills, Hyderabad and destinations across South India.
