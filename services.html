Our services

Taxi hire, tour packages and ticket booking from one trusted local operator in Puttaparthi.

🚖

Outstation taxi

Choose a one-way or round trip in any of our vehicles. Fares are per kilometre with a daily minimum, and tolls and parking are paid at actuals.

🛫

Airport transfers

We plan the start time around your flight and track arrivals for pickups. Call us for a fixed airport fare.

🏢

Corporate transport

Daily employee transport, guest pickups and event vehicles with a dedicated contact, monthly billing and GST invoices.

🛕

Temple packages

Vehicle, driver and route planning for temple visits, with waiting time for darshan and stops at nearby temples.

🏝️

Holiday packages

We plan the vehicle, sightseeing and stays for your vacation, and share a full price before you book.

✈️

Flight tickets

We book domestic and international flights at the airline fare plus a small service charge, and help with changes and cancellations.

🚆

Train tickets

We book train tickets on your behalf at the IRCTC fare plus a service charge, including Tatkal attempts where possible.

🚌

Bus tickets

Sleeper, semi-sleeper and seater buses with leading operators at the operator fare plus a service charge.

How ticket booking works

  1. Send us the travel date, route and passenger names on WhatsApp or by phone.
  2. We share the available options with the exact fare and our service charge.
  3. You confirm and pay by UPI, bank transfer or cash.
  4. We book the ticket and send it to you on WhatsApp or email, usually within 2 hours.
